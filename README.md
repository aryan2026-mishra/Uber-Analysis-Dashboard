# 🚗 Uber Analysis Dashboard

> An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Uber ride demand, revenue, trip patterns, peak hours, and geographic performance through dynamic filters and drill-down analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Uber Analysis Dashboard** transforms ride-level data into an interactive business intelligence solution for understanding transportation demand and revenue patterns.

The dashboard is organized into three major analytical areas:

1. **Overview Analysis**
2. **Time Analysis**
3. **Detailed Location & Trip Analysis**

Together, these views provide stakeholders with a structured way to explore ride demand, revenue, fares, locations, and time-based patterns.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

* How many rides are being completed?
* What is the overall revenue generated?
* What are the average fares?
* When is ride demand highest?
* Which days and hours generate the most rides?
* Which pickup and drop-off locations have higher demand?
* Which routes contribute significantly to ride activity?
* How does revenue change with ride demand?

---

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

### 1. 📈 Overview Page

Provides a high-level summary of overall ride performance.

### Key KPIs

* Total Rides
* Total Revenue
* Total Drivers
* Average Fare

The page also provides overall trend visualizations for quick performance monitoring.

---

### 2. ⏰ Time Analysis Page

Analyzes ride demand across different time dimensions.

The dashboard examines:

* Hourly demand
* Daily demand
* Monthly demand
* Weekday vs weekend patterns
* Peak ride periods

This analysis can support operational planning and help stakeholders understand when demand is concentrated.

---

### 3. 📍 Detail Analysis Page

Provides deeper analysis of:

* Pickup locations
* Drop-off locations
* Top routes
* Demand hotspots
* Trip-level information

The page includes drill-through functionality for deeper exploration of individual trip patterns.
